**Two discrepancies found in the paper:**
1. *Equation (8) has the wrong sign.* The text asserts an inhibitory effect of irrelevant drives, but the displayed formula reads `∂r/∂|h*ᵢ − hᵢ| > 0`. I proved the corrected statement (the reward strictly decreases in the irrelevant deprivation) and, separately, exhibited an explicit configuration with `n > m > 1` in which the derivative in question is strictly negative, refuting the printed inequality. The prose and the behavioural conclusion drawn from it are correct; only the printed sign is wrong.
2. *Equation (6) needs a no-overshoot hypothesis.* As printed it claims `∂r/∂k_j > 0` for every `k_j > 0` with no restriction on the internal state; this fails when the dose overshoots the setpoint (proved by an explicit instance with strictly negative derivative). I proved the claim on the range `0 ≤ k_j ≤ h*_j − h_j`.

Modelling choices: the derivative conditions (6)–(9) are formalized as the corresponding strict monotonicity / strict concavity statements (what the paper's prose asserts and its arguments use); the Proposition is formalized in the discrete form (S1)–(S5) of 'Materials and methods' rather than the informal integral form of Equations (3)–(5); (S6)–(S7) are stated for the one-dimensional quadratic drive, the case in which the paper derives them; the Taylor expansion of Equations (12)–(13), written with `≅`, is heuristic and is not formalized. All of this is documented in `README.md`.
