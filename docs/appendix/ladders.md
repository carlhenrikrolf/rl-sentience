# Ladders

The Cantril ladder is the questionaire item

> Please imagine a ladder with steps numbered from 0 at the bottom to 10 at the top. The top of the ladder represents the best possible life for you and the bottom of the ladder represents the worst possible life for you. On which step of the ladder would you say you personally feel you stand at this time?

asked by the World Happiness Report.
Many other questionaires use similar approaches, e.g.
the Subjective Happiness Scale,
the Positive and Negative Affect Schedule,
the Satisfaction with Life Scale,
Gallup's Positive Experience,
the Oxford Happiness Inventory.

**Definition** (Ladder).
A ladder is a mapping $G\rightarrow[0,1]$,
where $(G,\oplus,\preceq)$ is valence.
