
<details>
<summary>
<i>Notation</i> (L, W).
</summary>

We define
$p \mathbin{\mathsf L} q $
whenever
$p \mathbin{\underline{\mathsf L}} q$ and $p\neq q$,
and
$p \mathbin{\mathsf W} q $
whenever
$p \mathbin{\underline{\mathsf W}} q$ and $p\neq q$.


</details>



<details>
<summary>
<b>Definition</b> (Ambivalence).
</summary>

Let $(\mathcal V, \mathrm e, \div, \underline{\mathsf L})$ be a hedonic tone.
An *ambivalenced preference over $\mathcal V$* is tuple
$(\mathcal U, \underline{\mathsf W}, \mathsf A)$
where
$(\mathcal U, \underline{\mathsf W})$
is a preference over $\mathcal V$.
*Ambivalence*
$\mathsf A : \mathcal V^2 \to \mathcal V$.


1. $\left(\mathsf A \mathsf {\begin{bmatrix} p_1 \div q_1 \\ p_2 \div q_2 \end{bmatrix}} \right) \mathbin{\mathsf L} \mathrm e$
whenever
neither
${\begin{bmatrix}q_1 \\ p_2\end{bmatrix}} \mathbin{\mathsf W} {\begin{bmatrix}p_1 \\ q_2\end{bmatrix}}$
nor
${\begin{bmatrix}p_1 \\ q_2\end{bmatrix}} \mathbin{\mathsf W} {\begin{bmatrix}q_1 \\ p_2\end{bmatrix}}$.


</details>
